### Full description

Phoenix is a fitness app for people who want a simple place to begin. You do
not need gym knowledge, a perfect routine, or a room full of equipment.

Choose your goal, training location, available equipment, and the time that
realistically fits your life. Phoenix builds a clear plan and shows you the
next small step.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Train at home or at the gym with dumbbells, a bench, pull-up bar, bands, a treadmill, or no equipment  
• Receive exercises that match only the equipment you selected  
• Follow offline exercise images and clear technique guidance  
• Log sets, repetitions, loads, rest periods, and progress  
• Use a gentle daily check-in for energy, sleep, and fatigue  
• Get a suggested nutrition plan with calorie and macro targets  
• Track water and meals, with optional meal-photo capture  
• Review local progress reports and create an encrypted backup  
• Open optional Spotify and Apple Music links  
• Choose color themes, high contrast, and appearance settings

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account is required. Profiles, workouts, nutrition, weight, and photos stay
on the device. The current release contains no ads or tracking. Moving to a new
device happens only when you explicitly create and share a backup.

Phoenix is a general fitness and wellbeing tool. It is not a medical device and
does not replace advice from a qualified health professional.

### What’s new — 1.5.1

## Reviewer notes

Phoenix is a local-first fitness and nutrition app. No reviewer account or login
is required.

First-launch review path:

1. Choose a goal.
2. Choose Home or Gym and at least one equipment option, including “No equipment”.
3. Choose days, session duration, and experience level.
4. Enter height and weight to generate the local plan.

The reviewer may use non-personal test values. All resulting data remains on the
test device. Camera/photo access is optional and is requested only from the meal
photo feature. Notifications are optional local reminders. Denying either
permission does not block the core app.

The app contains no advertising, analytics SDK, mandatory account, remote user
database, or in-app purchase in this release. Optional Spotify, Apple Music,
exercise-video, and support links open external HTTPS destinations only after a
user action.

User-controlled export is available under `Me → Transfer to another device`.
The user chooses the destination of an encrypted backup. The app does not
silently upload health or fitness information.

Phoenix provides general fitness and wellbeing information. It is not a medical
device and does not diagnose, treat, or claim to prevent disease.
